Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to enter China?
It depends on your nationality. Many countries are eligible for 144-hour visa-free transit in Shenzhen. Your driver will provide guidance on visa policies and assist with entry forms.
What if my flight is delayed?
We monitor flight statuses in real-time. If your flight is delayed, we'll adjust the pickup time accordingly. The first 30 minutes of waiting is complimentary.
How do I pay for the service?
We accept credit card (via secure online payment), Alipay, WeChat Pay, and cash (HKD/RMB/USD). Payment details will be sent after booking confirmation.
Can I cancel or modify my booking?
Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking up to 24 hours before the pickup time for a full refund. Cancellations within 2 hours incur a $59 fee.
Is the price per person or per vehicle?
The price is per vehicle (7-seater Business MPV), not per person. You can book for 1–7 passengers. All passengers in your group travel together in the same vehicle.
What type of vehicle is used?
We use premium 7-seater Business MPVs — spacious, comfortable, and perfect for families or small groups with luggage. All vehicles are HK-licensed for direct border crossing.
