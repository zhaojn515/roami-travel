Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the free Starter Kit? +

The free Starter Kit includes step-by-step guides for WeChat Pay and Alipay setup, eSIM recommendations for China, must-have app list, offline maps prep guide, essential Mandarin phrases, and a Shenzhen arrival checklist. You can download the PDF directly or have it sent to your email.

How fast will I receive my paid itinerary? +

We aim to deliver your personalized itinerary within 24 hours of receiving your travel details. For most orders, you'll receive your plan the same day or next morning. If we can't deliver within 24 hours, you're eligible for a full refund.

What if I'm not satisfied with my itinerary? +

Every paid plan includes revisions. The Personalized Itinerary includes 1 revision and the Companion includes 2. Just reply to your delivery email with changes and we'll update your plan. If we can't deliver within 24 hours, you're eligible for a full refund.

Do you support payment methods other than Creem? +

Currently we process payments through Creem, which supports credit cards, debit cards, and other common payment methods. We're exploring additional options — feel free to reach out if you have a preferred payment method.

Can I request changes to my itinerary? +

Absolutely! The Personalized Itinerary includes 1 revision and the Shenzhen Companion includes 2 revisions. Just reply to your delivery email with the changes you'd like and our team will update your plan promptly.
