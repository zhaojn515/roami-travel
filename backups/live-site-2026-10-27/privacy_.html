Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Roami Travel Technologies ("Roami," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you use our website, products, and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Email address — when you contact us, request products, or subscribe to updates
  • Itinerary questionnaire responses — travel dates, interests, budget, travel style, group size, and other preferences you provide when ordering a personalized Live Itinerary
  • Communications — emails, messages, and other correspondence you send us
  • Order information — product selections, payment arrangements, and delivery details

We do not collect technical information such as IP addresses or browser fingerprints unless explicitly stated in this policy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver products and services you request (e.g., build and send your Live Itinerary)
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our content, products, and user experience
  • Send marketing emails — only if you explicitly subscribe; every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to our services without your consent.

3. Payment Information

Currently, payment arrangements are handled via email correspondence. We do not process credit card payments directly on our website.

If we integrate a payment service provider in the future (such as Stripe or PayPal), payment information will be processed by that provider. We will not store your credit card numbers or sensitive payment credentials.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website is a static site with minimal use of cookies or tracking technologies. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts.

If we add analytics tools (e.g., to understand site performance or user behavior), we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, obtain your consent.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

You may request deletion of your personal information by emailing [email protected]. We will process deletion requests within 30 days, subject to any legal or contractual obligations.

6. Third-Party Links

Our content may include links to third-party websites (e.g., Gaode Maps, 12306 train booking, government portals). These sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for their content or data handling. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Effective date" at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you via email or a prominent notice on our website.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:
