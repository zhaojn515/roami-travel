Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Companion and Personalized?+

The Companion is our premium plan: 4–7 days (vs 1–3), 12 walking routes, priority 2-hour support, 2 revisions, and offline maps. The Personalized plan is ideal for shorter trips at a lower price.

What are the walking routes like?+

Each route is a 1–3 hour walk through a specific neighborhood or theme (food street, art district, nature trail, etc.). You'll get turn-by-turn directions, stop recommendations, and photos.

What if I need changes?+

Your plan includes 2 full revisions. Reply to your delivery email with changes and we'll update your itinerary. Additional revisions available upon request.

How does priority support work?+

You'll get a dedicated WhatsApp number with our local team. Questions are answered within 2 hours during your trip — whether it's a restaurant recommendation or help with directions.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and other common payment methods through our secure payment processor Creem.
