Frequently Asked Questions

What information do you need from me?+

After payment, you'll receive a form asking for your travel dates, interests (food, culture, shopping, nature, etc.), budget level, mobility considerations, and any must-see spots. The more detail, the better your plan.

How many POIs will be included?+

A typical 1-day plan includes 5–8 POIs, 2-day includes 10–15, and 3-day includes 15–20+. Each POI includes name, address, map link, hours, and a brief description.

What if I need changes?+

Your plan includes 1 revision. Reply to your delivery email with the changes you'd like and we'll update your itinerary. Additional revisions can be arranged.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and other common payment methods through our secure payment processor Creem.
