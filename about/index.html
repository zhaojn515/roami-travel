About Roami Travel

Practical travel information and personalized itineraries for independent travelers exploring Shenzhen and China.

Who We Are

Roami Travel helps independent travelers navigate China with confidence. We provide practical travel information, personalized itineraries, and on-the-ground support — all researched and organized by our team.

We started Roami because we saw a gap: most China travel resources are either outdated guidebooks or fragmented blog posts. Independent travelers need accurate, up-to-date information that actually works — especially for logistics like payments, navigation, and local access. That's what we build.

Our Approach

We focus on three things:

  • Accuracy. We research every detail and mark when information was last checked. Policies change (visa rules, payment systems, transit apps) — we track the dates so you know what's current.
  • Troubleshooting. China's digital ecosystem is different. Alipay setup, WeChat Pay, VPN options, navigation apps — we walk you through the practical steps that generic guides skip.
  • Frictionless experience. Whether it's a free Starter Kit or a personalized Live Itinerary, we aim for smooth delivery and real support when you need it.

Our Research Process

All content is researched by our team. We maintain a database of foreigner-friendly locations with accurate addresses in Chinese and English, opening hours, and access tips.

Transparency note: Our on-the-ground verification program is currently in progress. We're systematically visiting and verifying POIs across Shenzhen, but this work is ongoing. Information is compiled from official sources, local partners, and traveler feedback — and we clearly mark when each item was last reviewed.

What We Offer

Free: China Starter Kit

A pre-departure prep pack covering Alipay/WeChat Pay setup, VPN options, SIM cards, essential apps, and cultural tips. Download it before you land so you're ready to go.

$19: Shenzhen Playbook

A curated guide to the best of Shenzhen for foreigners — researched POIs with addresses, neighborhood guides, restaurant picks, and transport tips. Regularly updated as the city changes.

$69: Personalized Live Itinerary

Tell us about your trip in a 2-minute questionnaire, and we'll build a custom mobile itinerary delivered in 24 hours. Includes navigation links, Chinese addresses for drivers, and curated recommendations matched to your interests. If we miss the 24h deadline, you get a full refund.

$129: Shenzhen Companion

Everything in Personalized, plus 7 days of WeChat concierge support. Get real-time help during your trip — photo translations, last-minute changes, local recommendations, or just asking "what should I eat tonight?"

Our Commitment

We're an independent team, not a large tour operator. We care about getting the details right because our reputation depends on it. If something in our content is outdated or incorrect, email us and we'll fix it. If you're not satisfied with a paid product, we'll make it right — that's our guarantee.

China travel is complicated, but it doesn't have to be overwhelming. We're here to make it simpler.
