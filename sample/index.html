Highlights

Iconic undulating roof design inspired by a flying phoenix. The building's dramatic curves and massive scale make it one of Shenzhen's most photographed landmarks. Best viewed from multiple angles—front, side, and especially from Lianhua Hill across the street.

Highlights

Learn Shenzhen's remarkable transformation from a small fishing village to a megacity of 17+ million people. The museum's permanent exhibitions cover local history, culture, and the economic reforms that created modern Shenzhen. Excellent English signage and interactive displays.

What to Avoid

Closed on Mondays. Allow at least 60 minutes for a proper visit. Photography is allowed but no flash.

Highlights

One of China's largest bookstores with an impressive multi-story layout. Perfect for browsing English books, picking up souvenirs, or escaping the heat/rain. The bookstore has a cozy café area and regular cultural events. Great place to spend 30 minutes browsing or picking up unique gifts.

Lianhua Hill Park

莲花山公园
Park
Address
Hongli Road, Futian District (600m from Civic Center Metro Station)
Metro
Civic Center Station or Lianhua North Station
Hours
Open 24 hours
Ticket
Free

Highlights

The crown jewel of this route. Hike to the hilltop plaza (easy 15-minute climb) for the best panoramic view of Shenzhen's CBD skyline. You'll see the Civic Center's iconic roof from above, the Ping An Tower (one of the world's tallest buildings), and the sprawling cityscape. Perfect spot for photos, especially in the morning light or at sunset.

What to Avoid

Not rain-friendly (no covered areas). Wear comfortable shoes for the hill climb. Bring water on hot days. The hilltop has no shade—visit in the morning or late afternoon to avoid midday heat.
